# Video masters

Video generation will move to a BB generation agent after its model and job
contract are chosen. Do not launch the legacy direct-provider video client
from this skill. Keep any existing job records for reconciliation; an
ambiguous old submission is not permission to submit it again.

For a future image-to-video request, prepare anchors derived from one master
so geometry, framing and exposure match. A video is a source artifact, not
automatically a production frame sequence. Record duration, resolution,
aspect ratio, audio requirement, reference images and budget before
dispatching a generation agent. Do not upload proprietary sources to public
hosts.

Review the full clip for camera drift, contour stability, lighting coherence
and actual movement through the [BB animation review](../references/animation-loop-review.md).
Send an accepted master to slot-spine-skin only when frame extraction or
rigging is requested. Continuous FX should first be considered for reusable
layers or mesh; any frame sequence must pass the target game's packed-texture
budget.
